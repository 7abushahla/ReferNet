# Reference timeline: Refer or Clear (started 10 October 2026)

Every source cited in `paper/main.tex`, with its first public appearance, its peer-reviewed version, the version actually read for this paper, and whether the claim we draw from it has been checked. Kept under the authors' standing rule (10 Oct 2026): track versions and dates for every source, never present older work as recent, and prefer the peer-reviewed version. arXiv identifiers encode the year and month of version 1 (YYMM).

Status words: **checked** (the number or claim was read in the source on the date given), **from notes** (taken from the plan, the study brief or the evidence summaries, not yet re-read in the source), **needs PDF** (the peer-reviewed version could not be accessed; the authors can download it into `/shared/b00088568/gdrfa_award_work/papers/`).

## 2. Groups whose work continues across the timeline

- **Idiap (Korshunov, Marcel, George):** FantasyID (arXiv July 2025) → the DeepID challenge (ICCV Workshops 2025) → EdgeDoc (arXiv August 2025). The benchmark, the challenge and a lightweight detector come from one group within a few months.
- **CVC Barcelona with IDnow and partners (Ramos Terrades, Boned, Talarmain; Awal, Ghanmi, Chiron):** CheckSim (2021) → Neitthoffer et al. (2023, with Awal) → SIDTD (2024) and the recurrent few-shot verifier (ICDAR 2024). The reference-based line and the SIDTD benchmark share authors.
- **da/sec, Darmstadt (Busch) and Tapia:** differential morphing-attack detection (Scherhag et al., 2020) → the ID-card PAD review (Ruiz et al., 2025) → prototypes of the target card for unseen countries (Nieto-Hidalgo, Espin and Tapia, 2026).
- **L3i, La Rochelle (Burie, Ming, Al-Ghadi, Coustaty):** MIDV-2020 (2021 to 2022, with Smart Engines) → guilloche verification against a reference (2022) → FMIDV (2023).
- **Smart Engines (Bulatov, Arlazarov):** MIDV-2020 → MIDV-Holo (2023) → the Smart ID Engine.
